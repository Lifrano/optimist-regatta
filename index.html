    Racing rules in the game

    • R10On opposite tacks, a port-tack boat (wind from the left) keeps clear.
    • R11On the same tack and overlapped, the windward boat keeps clear.
    • R12On the same tack and not overlapped, the boat clear astern keeps clear.
    • R13A boat that is tacking (past head to wind until close-hauled) keeps clear.
    • R18In the zone (3 boat lengths) the inside boat gets mark-room. Does not apply at the windward mark between boats on opposite tacks.
    • R29A boat over the line at the starting signal must return and start again.
    • R31Touching the next mark, the start buoy or the committee boat gives the 5-second penalty.
    • R15A boat that acquires right of way (e.g. right after a tack) must initially give the other boat room to keep clear.
    • R18.2bThe boat that reaches the zone clear ahead gets mark-room.
    • R18.3A boat that tacks inside the zone at the windward mark gets no mark-room and keeps clear.
    • R22A boat returning after a premature start keeps clear.
    • R28Fleet race: start – leave marks 1 and 2 to port – through gate 3s/3p (round 3s to starboard or 3p to port) – upwind finish between the blue buoy and the committee boat.
    • R44Instead of penalty turns: a boat that breaks a rule (contact, touching a mark) is much slower for exactly 5 seconds. During that time it is see-through and other boats sail straight through it.

    Team Race (Appendix D)

    • DTwo teams of 4 boats each. Course: start – marks 1 and 2 to starboard – marks 3 and 4 to port – upwind finish.
    • D1The zone is only 2 boat lengths.
    • D2Contact between boats of the same team: the umpires act without a protest. If a boat breaks a rule against an opponent, the opponent can protest. The protested boat has 3 seconds to take a One-Turn Penalty: one tack and one gybe. Otherwise the umpires come alongside in a small black motorboat: green-and-white flag = no penalty, red flag = Two-Turns Penalty: tack, gybe, tack, gybe. A tack must always be followed by a gybe and a gybe by a tack. Touching a mark: One-Turn Penalty.
    • D3Each boat scores its finishing place as points. The team with fewer points wins. On a tie, the team without first place wins. DNF, RET and DSQ score as many points as boats in the race.
    • D2.3A penalty that is not sailed within 60 seconds: the umpires show the black-and-white flag – disqualified (DSQ).
    • D3.1A boat that never starts correctly (OCS) scores 10 extra points.
